# Student Survey — VOYAGE / NearbyQuest

## Problem Statement
When students travel to a new city for internships, college fests, or trips,
they struggle to discover nearby places quickly, plan their time, and estimate
travel costs — all from a single tool.

## Survey Questions (Google Form — 8 responses collected)

**Q1. When visiting a new city, how do you currently find places to explore?**
- Google Maps: 6/8
- Instagram/YouTube: 4/8
- Ask friends: 5/8
- No plan: 2/8

**Q2. Do you find it hard to estimate cab/auto fares before travelling?**
- Yes, very difficult: 5/8
- Somewhat: 2/8
- No: 1/8

**Q3. Have you ever missed a good spot because you didn't know it existed nearby?**
- Yes: 7/8
- No: 1/8

**Q4. Would a single tool that shows nearby places, fares and itinerary help you?**
- Definitely yes: 7/8
- Maybe: 1/8
- No: 0/8

**Q5. Would you use a Book Ola/Uber directly button from a place card?**
- Yes: 8/8
- No: 0/8

## Key Quotes from Respondents
- I always waste 30 mins figuring out what's nearby when I reach a new place. — 3rd year CS student
- Knowing the auto fare before I go would save so much stress. — 2nd year student
- I missed the famous lake near my internship city because no one told me. — Final year student
- One app that combines discovery + booking would be a game changer. — 2nd year student
- Google Maps shows everything but doesn't tell me what's worth visiting. — 1st year student

## Summary
- 87.5% of students struggle to discover places in new cities
- 87.5% have missed a good spot due to lack of awareness
- 62.5% find fare estimation difficult
- 100% would use a direct Ola/Uber booking feature
- 87.5% said a combined discovery + itinerary + booking tool would help them
